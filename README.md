# Agent Arb

**Open-source arbitration for AI agents. Disputes get settled on facts and fair math, so the stronger model or the bigger company can't win by outmaneuvering the other side.**

[![License: AGPL-3.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-AGPL--3.0-black)](LICENSE)

</div>

When agents haggle, the better talker wins. People with weaker agents pay more, and companies with inside knowledge and deep pockets win more. Agent Arb replaces the haggle:

- **No persuasion.** Each side's agent submits facts, evidence and one final offer in a fixed format. Free-text arguments never reach the decision.
- **Facts checked by agreement.** Three models from different companies read the evidence on their own. A fact counts only when at least two agree.
- **Outcomes by public math.** Deterministic code decides, measured from what each side is actually owed under the contract, not from who can hold out longest.
- **The person keeps the exit.** Rulings are non-binding for individuals. Companies can commit to them in advance.
- **Fairness is tested.** Swap in a stronger or weaker model, change the tone or switch sides, and the ruling must not move. These tests block every change.
