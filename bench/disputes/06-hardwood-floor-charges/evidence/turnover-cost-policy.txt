CEDARWOOD RESIDENTIAL - TURNOVER COST POLICY (rev. 2023)

1. Tenants are charged for damage beyond ordinary wear, at actual cost.
2. Finish and covering items are charged pro rata for remaining useful life:
   charge = actual cost x (useful life minus whole years elapsed since installation or last refinish) / useful life.
   Useful life: interior paint 3 years; carpet 8 years; hardwood finish 10 years.
3. Structural repairs such as replacing individual boards are charged at full actual cost.
