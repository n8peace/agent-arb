SKYLARK AIR - CONDITIONS OF CARRIAGE (excerpt)

4.1 Fare types. Basic Economy fares are non-refundable and non-changeable after the 24-hour period in 4.2.
4.2 24-HOUR CANCELLATION. A booking made 7 or more days before departure may be cancelled within 24 hours of the time of booking for a full refund of the fare to the original form of payment. This applies to all fare types, including Basic Economy.
