HEARTHSIDE HOME - RETURN AND DAMAGE POLICY

1. Change of mind: within 30 days of delivery. Customer pays return pickup ($95) and a 15% restocking fee on the item price. Delivery fee is not refunded.
2. Damaged or defective items: report within 7 days of delivery. Hearthside collects the item at no charge and refunds the item price and the delivery fee in full.
3. Signing the delivery receipt confirms delivery of the goods, not their condition. Condition claims are decided on inspection.
