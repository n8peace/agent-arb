FITNEST GYMS - ANNUAL MEMBERSHIP TERMS (version in effect 2025-01-01)

1. Annual plan: $468 paid up front ($39 per month), covering 12 monthly periods beginning on the 1st of each month.
2. Annual fees are non-refundable, except as stated in section 3.
3. RELOCATION. A member who moves 25 or more miles from every FitNest club may cancel by written notice with proof of the new address. The notice period is 30 days from the day FitNest receives the notice. FitNest refunds $39 for each full monthly period that begins after the notice period ends, less a $25 administrative fee.
